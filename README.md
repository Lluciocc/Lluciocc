## About

I am **Lluciocc**, a French computer science student and open-source developer focused on building polished, useful software from product idea to release. My work spans **desktop engineering, full-stack development, developer tooling, multimedia systems, and low-level experimentation**, with a strong preference for local-first applications and thoughtful user experiences.

I build primarily with **Python, C, C#, TypeScript, GTK, Qt, React, Node.js, .NET, GStreamer, and SQLite**. Beyond implementation, I care about architecture, packaging, cross-platform delivery, CI, accessibility, documentation, and the small product decisions that make software enjoyable to use every day.

My AI/ML experience includes applied deep-learning exploration and an internship at **Digiactivity**, while projects such as Groovia let me explore signal analysis, metadata enrichment, ranking, and algorithmic automation in a real product context.

### Open To

- Open-source collaboration on Linux, GNOME, developer tooling, and systems projects
- Software engineering and applied AI/ML learning opportunities
- Product-focused projects that combine engineering, design, and practical impact
- Contributions involving desktop applications, multimedia, automation, or developer experience
